# Brand & logo

## Logo assets required

The supplied logo is a raster illustration on a photographic flag background. That works as a social image but not as a website logo. Before launch, the federation should provide (or commission) these vector versions:

| Asset | Contents | Used in |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Crest, full | Coat of arms, laurels, athletes, bands, "FDFF" on a transparent background | Landing hero, footer, results page header, printable documents |
| Crest, compact | Laurels + athletes + bands + "FDFF" (no coat of arms) | Header on tablet/desktop, login screen |
| Wordmark | "FDFF" in the logo's serif, white or blue | Mobile header, admin sidebar |
| Monogram | "FDFF" in a blue square | Favicon (32 px), app icon, avatar placeholder |

Until the vectors exist, use the current image cropped to the crest, on the dark background only.

## Usage rules

- **Clear space:** at least the height of the "F" in "FDFF" on every side.
- **Minimum size:** full crest 96 px wide; compact crest 40 px tall; wordmark 20 px tall.
- **Backgrounds:** use the crest on `--color-bg` or `--color-surface`. On photographs, place it on a dark overlay.
- **Never:** recolor the laurels or bands, stretch the crest, add drop shadows or glows, place it on the red action color, or recreate "FDFF" in the UI typeface. The serif "FDFF" belongs to the logo only.
- **Official name** on first mention in a page or document: Federación Dominicana de Fisicoculturismo y Fitness (FDFF). Use "FDFF" afterwards. Do not use "FDFF Digital".
